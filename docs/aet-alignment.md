# AET alignment (proposed, not accredited)

> **Requires review by an accredited AET specialist.** This document is generated from the content files by `npm run docs:aet`; do not edit it by hand.
>
> PROPOSED MAPPING ONLY. These outcome descriptors were drafted by the product team to help organisations see how lessons relate to Adult Education and Training (AET) levels. They are not official curriculum statements, no SAQA unit standards have been confirmed, and nothing here implies accreditation. Every mapping requires review by an accredited AET specialist before use in any funding, certification or reporting context.

Status: 0 of 12 outcome descriptors have been reviewed by an accredited AET specialist. Until an outcome is reviewed, every report, export and certificate that mentions it carries the words "proposed mapping, not accredited".

## How the levels relate

| App level | Title | Proposed AET level | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Home-language reading bridge | pre-aet | Decoding in isiZulu or Sesotho first: home-language vowels, syllables and first words, then carrying the skill into English. Optional; offered when the placement shows the learner cannot yet decode in their home language. |
| 1 | Foundations | aet-1 | English letter recognition, phonemic awareness, letter-sound relationships and first letter formation. |
| 2 | First words | aet-1 | Blending sounds into words, word identification, spelling with tiles, picture matching and common sight words. |
| 3 | Sentences | aet-1 | Reading simple sentences, building sentences and basic comprehension. |
| 4 | Everyday English | aet-2 | Reading SMS messages, signs, notices and other everyday texts, and typing short replies. |
| 5 | Independent reading | aet-2 | Reading short passages, answering questions and writing short responses. |
